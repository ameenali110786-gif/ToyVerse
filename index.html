A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Curious? Good.

Here are a few things people often wonder about.

Are these real products and prices? +

This is a client-ready demo catalogue. Product details, PKR prices, ratings, stock and discounts are fictional examples and must be verified by the store owner before launch.

Can I place an order on this website? +

You can add products to the demo cart and prepare a WhatsApp inquiry once a store phone number is configured. Checkout, payment processing and order storage are not connected.

How do I ask about a specific toy? +

Use the quick-view or cart controls to review a toy. Configure your WhatsApp number in app.js to enable order inquiries with selected product details.

Can I change the branding? +

Yes. Edit the STORE settings at the top of app.js to change the brand name, WhatsApp number, currency display and contact details.
